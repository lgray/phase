## 0. Premise verification (Step 0)

Rule text, verbatim from the brief's quoted Dandan article (`brief/issue-5169.md` line 33): "When taking mulligans, if a player draws a hand with less than two lands or spells, that player may reveal the hand and take a free mulligan. This no longer applies once that player has taken a regular mulligan." Settled decision 1 (binding, same file): "a hand qualifies when it has fewer than two lands OR fewer than two nonland cards, before that player's first regular mulligan." The article says "lands or spells"; the settled decision reads "spells" as "nonland cards", which is the engine's `FreeRevealMulligan::WhenHandLacks { min_lands: 2, min_nonlands: 2 }` (Phase 2, `types/format.rs`, read).

Two readings the article leaves open, decided here and stated so reviewers can overrule them:
1. **Repeatable.** "This no longer applies once that player has taken a regular mulligan" and a free mulligan is not a regular one, so a player whose redrawn hand again qualifies may free-reveal again; the permission ends only when a regular mulligan is taken (count leaves 0). The charter's verification phrase "may FreeReveal once" is satisfied as "once per qualifying hand".
2. **Reveal timing.** The reveal belongs to the free mulligan, which CR 103.5 carries out at the close of the declare round ("all players who decided to take mulligans do so at the same time"; Phase 12 3.4). The reveal is therefore published at the close, before any hand is returned, so a seat declaring later never sees an earlier declarer's hand.

Cards used by rows, Oracle text fetched from Scryfall `cards/named?exact=` on 2026-10-01 and matching `client/public/card-data.json` (jq) verbatim:
- Island (Basic Land, Island): "({T}: Add {U}.)"
- Dandân (Creature, Fish): "This creature can't attack unless defending player controls an Island.\nWhen you control no Islands, sacrifice this creature."
- Mystic Sanctuary (Land, Island): "({T}: Add {U}.)\nThis land enters tapped unless you control three or more other Islands.\nWhen this land enters untapped, you may put target instant or sorcery card from your graveyard on top of your library."
- Serum Powder (Artifact): "{T}: Add {C}.\nAny time you could mulligan and this card is in your hand, you may exile all the cards from your hand, then draw that many cards. (You can do this in addition to taking mulligans.)"
The Dandan decklist (brief lines 47-52) holds 34 lands (20 Island, 2 each of Mystic Sanctuary, Halimar Depths, Haunted Fengraf, Lonely Sandbar, Remote Isle, The Surgical Bay, Svyelunite Temple) and 46 nonland cards, so every composition row below is drawable from the real pile. Serum Powder is not in that list (charter Phase 13 text); it appears only in the precedence row.

## 2. Pattern Coverage (assessed against the charter's class attribution)

Class: every format whose `free_reveal_mulligan()` axis is `WhenHandLacks { min_lands, min_nonlands }` gets a free reveal mulligan with those thresholds, over either timing (`Immediate` per-player libraries, `Simultaneous` shared pile) and either deal order. Dandan is the only such format today; the threshold pair and the axis are the extension point, so a future format with other thresholds (or a `Custom(_)` source that can express the axis) inherits legality, the declare-round participation, the AI selection and the client button with no code change. The hand predicate is parameterized by the axis, not written as "two" anywhere in `mulligan.rs`. The AI and client are consumers of the engine-enumerated candidate and carry no format identity.

## 3. Design

### 3.1 Wire: `MulliganChoice::FreeReveal`

`types/actions.rs`: append a unit variant `FreeReveal` after `UseSerumPowder` (appended so the derived `Ord` that `action_stable_order.rs` uses for candidate sorting keeps every existing ordering). Doc: "CR 103.5 as modified by the Dandan free-reveal rule: reveal the hand, return it, redraw; the mulligan count is unchanged and nothing is bottomed. Legal only where `GameFormat::free_reveal_mulligan()` offers it, before this player's first regular mulligan and while the hand qualifies." The enum's header doc (the `Keep`/`Mulligan`/`UseSerumPowder` list) gains the entry. Serialization is the existing `#[serde(tag = "type", content = "data")]`, so the unit variant is `{ "type": "FreeReveal" }`. No payload: it needs no object reference.

### 3.2 Availability is computed by one engine authority, not stored on the entry (charter divergence, stated and reasoned)

The charter entry says availability "rides the entry or `MulliganDecisionPhase` as a typed value, never a `bool`". This plan does **not** add a stored field, for three measured reasons, and instead gives the engine one function whose result every consumer reads:

```rust
/// CR 103.5 as modified by the Dandan free-reveal rule: whether `entry`'s seat may take the free reveal mulligan now.
pub(crate) fn free_reveal_offered(state: &GameState, entry: &MulliganDecisionEntry) -> bool
```
- Leak (measured): `game/visibility.rs` does not redact `WaitingFor::MulliganDecision` (it appears only in the paid-cast "complete rather than catch-all" list, which names it as carrying no paid-cast authority), so a stored availability on `pending` entries would be sent to the opponent as "this hand has fewer than two lands or nonlands" before any reveal. The rule reveals the hand only when the player chooses to. A computed value is exposed solely through the owner-scoped candidate list (`candidate_actions_for_semantic_owner_with_probe` retains only that owner's candidates; read).
- Staleness: the hand changes at four places (opening deal, Serum Powder redraw, the Regular close, the FreeReveal close), and Phase 12 rebuilds entries in two of them; a stored value must be recomputed at each or it lies. A computed value cannot drift.
- Churn: an entry field forces every `MulliganDecisionEntry { .. }` literal (12 files at charter time, `git grep -l 'MulliganDecisionEntry {' -- crates`) to change, which Phase 12 deliberately avoided.
The charter's own verification plan reads the same: the GamePage test renders "an engine-enumerated `MulliganDecision { choice: FreeReveal }` candidate", i.e. availability reaches the UI as a candidate. The "typed value, never a `bool`" rule governs data fields; here the data is the typed axis `FreeRevealMulligan` (Phase 2) read at call time, and the predicate returns `bool` as any `is_*`/`*_offered` predicate does. If the orchestrator insists on a stored carrier the fallback is a typed field on `MulliganDecisionEntry` with per-viewer redaction added to `visibility.rs`; that is a charter revision (extra paths: `visibility.rs` and its tests) and is not planned.

Body (all in `game/mulligan.rs`, beside `free_first_mulligan`):
```rust
match state.format_config.format.free_reveal_mulligan() {
    FreeRevealMulligan::Unavailable => false,
    FreeRevealMulligan::WhenHandLacks { min_lands, min_nonlands } => {
        let (lands, nonlands) = hand_land_split(state, entry.player);
        matches!(entry.phase, MulliganDecisionPhase::Declare)
            && entry.mulligan_count == 0
            && (lands < usize::from(min_lands) || nonlands < usize::from(min_nonlands))
    }
}
```
with `hand_land_split` counting the seat's hand objects by `card_types.core_types.contains(&CoreType::Land)` (CR 205.2a: land is a card type; `nonland` is every other card, so Mystic Sanctuary, whose Oracle text above shows an Island land, counts as a land and Dandân as a nonland). Exhaustive `match`, no wildcard.

`mulligan_count == 0` is the "no regular mulligan yet" test: measured by reading the `Mulligan` arm, `new_count = current_count + 1` increments unconditionally, and `free_first` only changes `bottom_count_for` and the cap, so the count is 0 exactly until a regular mulligan or a Tiny Leaders forced mulligan (which seeds the count from the prepaid ledger) happens. This holds for any seat count and either `free_first_mulligan` value, which is stronger than the charter's "relies on Dandan being 2-player only"; the claim is still re-read at phase start.

### 3.3 Handler arm and the two timings

`handle_mulligan_decision`, new arm (after `UseSerumPowder`, exhaustive match unchanged otherwise):
```rust
MulliganChoice::FreeReveal => {
    if !free_reveal_offered(state, &pending[idx]) {
        return Err(format!("Player {player:?} may not take a free reveal mulligan"));
    }
    match mulligan_timing(state) {
        MulliganTiming::Immediate => redraw_after_free_reveal(state, player, events),
        MulliganTiming::Simultaneous => {
            // CR 103.5: record the declaration; the reveal and redraw happen at the round's close.
            let entry = pending.remove(idx);
            declared.push(MulliganDeclaration { player, mulligan_count: entry.mulligan_count, kind: MulliganDeclarationKind::FreeReveal });
        }
    }
}
```
`redraw_after_free_reveal(state, player, events)` = `reveal_hand` + `shuffle_hand_into_library` + `draw_n(.., STARTING_HAND_SIZE, ..)`; it changes no count and no ledger, so the entry stays in `Declare` (the `Immediate` arm; today unreachable by any registered format because the only free-reveal format shares its library, but the match over the timing is exhaustive and the helper is the whole arm, unit-tested directly, V7). Existing `Regular` declarations in Phase 12's `Mulligan` arm gain `kind: MulliganDeclarationKind::Regular`.

Phase 12's hand stability invariant makes validate-at-declare sufficient: between a declaration and the close, a held seat's hand cannot change (a held seat is not in `pending`, so it cannot use Powder, V4e of Phase 12; other seats' actions move cards only between their own hands and the pile). `close_declare_round` therefore does not re-validate.

`types/game_state.rs` (Phase 12's `MulliganDeclaration`):
```rust
/// CR 103.5: what a held mulligan does when the declare round closes.
#[derive(Debug, Clone, Copy, PartialEq, Eq, Default, Serialize, Deserialize)]
#[serde(tag = "type")]
pub enum MulliganDeclarationKind { #[default] Regular, FreeReveal }
```
and `MulliganDeclaration` gains `#[serde(default)] pub kind: MulliganDeclarationKind`. `MulliganDecisionEntry` and `MulliganDecisionPhase` are **not changed** (diff empty).

### 3.4 The close: one exhaustive match on the kind

`close_declare_round` (Phase 12 3.4), with the changes:
1. `redrawers` ordered by `apnap_order_from(state, None, state.active_player)` as before.
2. **Reveal first.** For each redrawer whose kind is `FreeReveal`, in that order, `reveal_hand(state, player, events)` emits one `GameEvent::CardsRevealed { player, card_ids, card_names }` for the hand (CR 701.20a; the cards do not move, CR 701.20b; no `revealed_cards` set is written because the hand leaves the zone in this same close). All reveals precede every return, so the simultaneity of CR 103.5 is preserved.
3. Return every redrawer's hand, then shuffle each distinct container once, then `deal_hands` interleaved: unchanged from Phase 12 and shared by both kinds (this is where Phase 6's deferred FreeReveal shuffle leg lands: a non-canonical seat's FreeReveal shuffles the shared pile through the holder-seat shuffle, V3).
4. **Per-kind count, the single exhaustive match:**
```rust
let new_count = match declaration.kind {
    MulliganDeclarationKind::Regular => { state.prepaid_mulligan_bottoms.insert(player, 0); declaration.mulligan_count + 1 }
    MulliganDeclarationKind::FreeReveal => declaration.mulligan_count, // CR 103.5 + Dandan rule: not a regular mulligan; nothing owed
};
```
The rebuilt entry is `{ player, mulligan_count: new_count, phase: Declare }`; the cap check `new_count >= max_mulligans_for(free_first)` stays universal (a FreeReveal count of 0 never reaches it). A FreeReveal seat therefore returns to `Declare` at count 0 with a new hand, may free-reveal again if that hand qualifies (reading 1), and owes no bottoms.

### 3.5 AI: select the engine-issued candidate, never re-derive the predicate

- `ai_support/candidates.rs` `Declare` arm: after the `Mulligan` candidate, `if mulligan::free_reveal_offered(state, entry) { push candidate(MulliganDecision { choice: FreeReveal }, TacticalClass::Selection, Some(entry.player)) }`. The doc comment above the arm gains the FreeReveal sentence.
- phase-ai `search.rs::deterministic_choice`, `Declare` branch only (the edit stays out of `large_board_main_phase_has_no_development_sources`, Phase 17's function): the verdict ladder becomes `decision.keep` then Keep; else the first engine-issued FreeReveal found in the `actions` slice (`actions.iter().any(|a| matches!(a, GameAction::MulliganDecision { choice: MulliganChoice::FreeReveal }))`; `actions` is the contract's owner-scoped domain, so another seat's candidate cannot appear); else Serum Powder if in hand; else Mulligan. Precedence FreeReveal over Powder (the order the charter asks this plan to state): FreeReveal is free and repeatable while the hand qualifies, a Powder is a one-shot card, so the Powder is kept for a later decision. Unobservable in Dandan (no Powder in the list); pinned by V9 so the rule is not accidental. When the registry keeps, the AI keeps whether or not FreeReveal is offered. Empty `actions` (the rollout call sites pass `&[]`) yields no FreeReveal and falls to Powder/Mulligan exactly as today.
- `search.rs::fallback_action`, `Declare` branch: FreeReveal when issued (`issued(...)` over `contract.candidates`, already gated by `gate`), else the existing Powder-else-Keep ladder. This changes the Powder-first order of the existing branch to FreeReveal-first, consistent with the rule above.
- Liveness: a FreeReveal leaves the count at 0, so `card_floor.rs` (which reads `kept_hand_size_after(mulligans_taken + 1, ..)` and is therefore not reached at count 0; read, lines 48-100) never force-keeps; termination rests on the redrawn hand eventually failing the predicate or the registry keeping. For the Dandan pile the per-hand qualification probability is a few percent (34 lands in 80), so a chain is short; no cap is added because the rules impose none. Hypothesis, not measurement; V9 includes a bounded-loop probe.
- `policies/mulligan/*`: unchanged (Phase 3's re-gate lets the registry decide for Dandan).
