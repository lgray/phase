## Determinize ruling (orchestrator: fallback binding)

`unknown_slots` feeds `determinize_opponents`, which pairs its slots with `unknown_hidden_pool(&sim, opponent, ..)` (a `deck_pools.iter().find(pool.player == opponent)`, empty when none). With Phase 6 provisioning (one pool, the canonical seat's):

- opponent = canonical seat: `players[opponent].library` already is the pile; `library_of(opponent)` returns the same container. The swap is the identity. (This path already resamples the pile when the AI is the non-canonical seat; that is pre-existing, and resampling is correct for the pile since CR 401.2 hides it from both seats. It is not a leak and not a double count: slot ids are distinct objects and 2-seat Dandan has one opponent.)
- opponent = non-canonical seat: the pool lookup is empty, so the loop `continue`s before slots are used. The swap is unobservable until Phase 17 converts the pool lookup.

So the fallback is rules- and AI-correct for Dandan: the opponent's library IS the pile, the direction of the change is the right one (once the pool resolves, the pile is treated as unknown to the AI instead of leaking its real order), and it introduces no leak or double count. Raw-keep with a pin is NOT preferable: the charter's Phase 9 scope names `determinize.rs`, and its deferral line covers only "AI heuristic read swaps and their semantic fixes (-> Phase 17)", which does not name this site (Phase 17's five-site list and scope rule exclude it). Fallback is the in-charter form.

Residuals the plan must name (F2): (a) `unknown_slots`' `obj.owner == opponent` filter drops pile cards owned by the other seat; once Phase 17 resolves the pool, for opponent = non-canonical seat the pile (owned by the canonical seat until Phase 11 rebinds on hand entry) is filtered out and keeps its real identities to the AI. That is the same species as the `deck_knowledge.rs` owner filter Phase 17 drops, but Phase 17's frozen scope rule does not list `determinize.rs`. (b) After the Phase 9 swaps, `card_name_choice_candidates` for AI = non-canonical seat reads names from the determinized (resampled) pile in each sample, while the module doc promises the AI's own zones are byte-identical across samples; this is a Phase 17 observation, not a Phase 9 fix.
