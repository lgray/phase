## 5. The rule, derived from CR 613.8b

Nodes are the bucket's effects sorted by `(!characteristic_defining, timestamp, source_id, def_index, mod_index)`; edge `j -> i` means `i` depends on `j` (`depends_on(i, j)`), computed once (CR 613.8c stays satisfied exactly as today, because `depends_on` is state-blind).

1. Repeatedly apply the lowest-ranked unprocessed node whose dependencies are all applied (CR 613.8b "waits to apply until just after all of those effects"; ties resolved by timestamp, CR 613.8b "applied in timestamp order relative to each other").
2. When no such node exists, every remaining source strongly connected component is a nontrivial dependency loop (a singleton source would have in-degree 0 and would have been chosen in step 1; edges are unique per ordered pair and self-pairs are skipped, so in-degree counts exactly the unprocessed dependencies). Pick the source component (no edge into it from another unprocessed component) whose lowest-ranked member ranks lowest; delete the edges internal to it (each deleted edge lowers its target's in-degree once); resume step 1. CR 613.8b "the effects in the dependency loop are applied in timestamp order": after deletion every member has in-degree 0, so they are chosen by rank. Nodes that depend on loop members keep their in-degree from the loop and wait until the members they depend on have been applied.
3. Stalls can recur (a second loop, or a loop downstream of the first); each recurrence repeats step 2 on what remains.

Termination: after step 2, every member of the chosen component is unprocessed with in-degree 0 (no external incoming edge by the source property, internal edges deleted), so step 1 always makes progress; the number of processed nodes strictly increases each cycle of the loop.

Whole-loop bucket: the source component is the entire bucket, all edges are internal and deleted, so the result is pure rank order: identical to today's fallback.

Reading left open by the rule, stated so the executor does not guess: a dependent that depends on only some loop members becomes eligible as soon as those are applied, and then applies at its own rank among the eligible nodes (so it may apply between two loop members). This is "just after all effects it depends on" read literally; Phase 5's three-Hack case (B depends on A only; A and C form a loop) yields A, B, C, which equals the result of the stricter reading A, C, B, so no row in this plan or in Phase 5 separates the two readings. A loop member that also depends on an effect outside the loop waits for that effect (ordinary Kahn).
