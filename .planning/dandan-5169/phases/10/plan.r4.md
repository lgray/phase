Mode: engine-planner, phase-plan mode, phase k=10, revision 3 (the caller-claimed zone layer is deleted; Unit A is re-designed; Unit B is unchanged). Planned against W HEAD `e0a2dfdaa2` (upstream merge on candidate `9287a640b9`; phase base `1acc74bca6`). Every dynamic claim below was run on a scratch `git archive` copy with its own target dir (`source cargo-isolate.sh`, `MTGJSON_SKIP_REFRESH=1`), none in W; the prototype is a reference, not binding: `dandan-run/scripts/p10_prototype.diff` (production files, W HEAD to prototype). ## A. What this design closes, by construction

The licence is one rule: **a player-axis comparison (typed `controller`, `Owned`) against a card admits every seat reading the card's container only when the door states the zone the card is judged in: the owner-zone door states the card's own zone (CR 109.5), the departure door and the record door state the zone the card left (`record.from_zone`, CR 603.10a for a graveyard departure, CR 400.1 as the format modifies it for a library departure), and a filter that itself names the card's zone with `InZone`/`InAnyZone` states it; the card's zone alone is never a licence; a zone whose storage seat is the seat itself (every per-seat zone, every non-shared format) is the identity.** Nothing is claimed by a caller and no zone is injected into any filter, so no door can claim a zone the card was not in.

| Past finding | Door that now reads the real zone | Closing row |
|---|---|---|
| impl r1: mass `ChangeZoneAll` with the zone coming from the effect's `origin` | `change_zone::resolve_all` judges each candidate through `matches_target_filter_for_zone(obj.zone)`, the owner-zone door, which licenses with the object's own zone | V17 (Release to Memory), V21 |
| impl r2: trigger-origin `Owned`-form members and `ZoneChange*ThisTurn` `from` | record door: `licensed = record.from_zone`, no list | V18, V19 |
| impl r3: controller-form members at the record door | the controller arm and the `Owned` arm of a door read the SAME `licensed` value | V20 |
| impl r4: the `to == Battlefield` branch of `zone_change_clause_matches` | live entrant judged by `matches_target_filter_on_departure(record.from_zone)`; enters-triggers keep the plain door (CR 603.6a) | V19 reanimation legs, V22, V23 |
| impl r5: a mixed `[Battlefield, X]` claim collapsed a battlefield resident | there is no list; each tracked-set member is judged by its own zone, and a battlefield member is judged by the plain door | V21 |
| round-4 Standard regressions (three tests) from an injected `InZone` | nothing is injected; per-seat zones are the identity in `zone_axis_admits` | the three named tests in V13 |

Member classes the claim layer covered, and the door each now reads: `ChangeZoneAll` mass origin and `resolution_zone_candidates` (owner-zone door, per object); `ZoneChangeCountThisTurn` / `ZoneChangeAggregateThisTurn` `from` (record door); trigger origin `Equals`/`OneOf`, controller form and `Owned` form (record door when the card is not a live entrant, departure door when it is); the `to == Battlefield` branch (departure door, gated); the controller arm versus the `Owned` arm (one `licensed` per door); mixed claims (no list). Members that never had a claim (`Hand`, `Battlefield`, `Exile` origins) stay per-seat by the identity rule and by the licence rule.

## B. Decisions

**(a) Which seam needs what.**

| Seam | Judges | Door and zone read |
|---|---|---|
| `change_zone::resolve_all` (mass), `change_zone::resolution_zone_candidates` | live cards in the scanned zones | `matches_target_filter_for_zone(obj.zone)`: owner-zone door for Hand/Library/Graveyard, plain door otherwise |
| `trigger_matchers::zone_change_clause_matches`, card not a live entrant | the zone-change record | `matches_target_filter_on_zone_change_record`; the door reads `record.from_zone` |
| same, live entrant (`to == Battlefield`) whose trigger names an origin and does not name `Battlefield` as destination | the live permanent as it was in the departed zone | new `matches_target_filter_on_departure(state, id, from, filter, ctx)`; `from = record.from_zone` |
| same, live entrant otherwise (enters triggers, origin-less triggers) | the permanent | plain `matches_target_filter` (CR 603.6a) |
| `quantity::resolve_ref` `ZoneChangeCountThisTurn`, `ZoneChangeAggregateThisTurn` | records | record door; the `with_claimed_zones` lines are deleted |
| `casting.rs` | none | the `claimed_zones: &[]` struct literals are deleted (compiler-forced) |
| every other plain-door scanner of a pile zone (cost exiles, `bounce`, `dig`, `seek`, `reveal_until`, layers, replacements, casting permissions, trigger observers) | live cards | unchanged plain door: licensed only when the filter names the zone; an unnamed bare leaf stays per-seat |

The scanners in the last row are the class "a seam scans a pile zone with the plain door". Probe: the exile-from-graveyard cost filters of supported cards with `controller: You` all carry `InZone Graveyard` (python over `client/public/card-data.json` x `coverage-data.json`, cost `Exile` with `zone Graveyard`: every `controller You` row has `InZone`), so the filter-named leg already licenses them and they need no edit. A scanner whose filter does not name the zone and whose leaf is not pile residency (an observer, a permission gate) is refused on purpose; owner-gated permissions are Phase 11's.

**(b) Does the licence still need the filter's own zone claim, or may it key on the actual zone?** It may not key on `obj.zone` alone. Population: every distinct (door, zone, filter shape, caller) evaluating a filter with a controller or `Owned` leaf and no filter-named zone against a Graveyard/Library object or record, over the whole `phase-engine` nextest suite on a scratch copy carrying the census patch `dandan-run/scripts/p10_census_emit.patch` (run: `CENSUS_FILE=<tsv> cargo nextest run -p phase-engine`). The callers found are off-zone keyword effects, graveyard cast and land-play permissions, `layers` conditions, `replacement` valid-card checks, trigger observers (`target_filter_matches_object`, `valid_card_matches`, `valid_source_matches`), and the mass/resolution seams. Only the mass and resolution seams and the zone-change records carry pile-residency semantics; every other caller is an observer or a gate whose leaf means "this player's", which is what the three Standard regressions of round 4 and the Sacrificed/Milled observers (V14, V16) show. So the licence needs a typed signal. The filter-named leg is load-bearing: removing it (mutation N2, scratch copy, `nextest -E 'test(dandan) or test(oglor) or test(opponent_qualified) or test(logical_zone_production)'`) fails the Phase 8 test `bounce::dandan_read_sweep_tests::chosen_non_canonical_player_returns_a_card_from_the_shared_pile_graveyard` and the filter-named unit legs, whose seam is a plain-door scan with a zone-naming filter. This does not change a charter decision: S2c's text (`grep -n "Phase 10 — S2c" phase-charter`, the Goal paragraph) says "for an object in a shared zone ... treat the axis as satisfied for every seat" and states no licence key; the typed signal decides which callers are asking the pile-residency question and is plan-level.

## 2. Pattern Coverage

Class: every Oracle text whose "your/their/an opponent's graveyard or library" is judged in a format with a shared library or graveyard (`GameFormat::shared_zones()`; Dandan today), through four doors: the live plain door, the owner-zone door, the record door and the departure door, plus the count arms of Unit B. The 23-card Dandan list is the acceptance floor; the class is every supported card whose typed `controller` or `Owned` leaf is evaluated against a pile card or a pile-origin record. Figures regenerate by command, not by edited number: the trigger-side population by `python3 dandan-run/scripts/p10_trigger_census.py buckets` (run from `client/public`; supported cards x ChangesZone/ChangesZoneAll triggers and their `zone_change_clauses`), the evaluator-side population by the census patch in B(b), the count-arm populations by a walk over `card-data.json` x `coverage-data.json` for the named `QuantityRef`.

Supported-card census of pile-origin to battlefield triggers (`p10_trigger_census.py pile-enters`): every member's `valid_card` has no bare controller/`Owned` leaf, so the departure gate (V23) has no real member today; it is the rule CR 603.6a requires, kept because the unguarded branch would collapse a permanent's controller for any future such trigger.

## 3. Design

### 3.1 Unit A: the licence at the doors (game/filter.rs and the seams)

1. `ControllerLookup` (private, `Copy`, already threaded through every recursion site of the live door) gains `OwnerZone` (owner-zone door: the card is judged as a resident of its own owner-scoped zone) and `Departed(Zone)` (departure door: the card is judged as of the zone it left). `LiveOnly` stays for `matches_target_filter_including_phased_out` and `matches_target_filter_on_event_snapshot`, which must not gain a licence from the door.
2. `FilterContext::claimed_zones`, `FilterContext::with_claimed_zones`, `claimed_shared_zone`, the `claimed_zones` parameter threaded through the filter recursion, every `claimed_zones: &[]` literal (in `filter.rs` and `casting.rs`) and the `with_claimed_zones` calls in `quantity.rs` are deleted. Acceptance for the deletion: `grep -rn "claimed_zones\|with_claimed_zones\|claimed_shared_zone\|claim_scan_zones" crates` has no hit.
3. `resident_zone(properties, obj_zone, lookup) -> Option<Zone>` is the single authority for the live door: `Departed(from)` gives `Some(from)`, `OwnerZone` gives `Some(obj_zone)`, `LiveOrLki | LiveOnly` give `Some(obj_zone)` only when the filter's own `InZone`/`InAnyZone` names `obj_zone`. Its result is the one `licensed` value the typed-controller arm and the `Owned` arm both read (via `zone_axis_admits` and `owned_axis_admits`), so the arms cannot diverge.
4. The record door (`zone_change_filter_inner`) reads `let licensed = record.from_zone;` and feeds it to both arms. A record whose `from_zone` is `None`, `Battlefield`, `Hand` or `Exile` yields no collapse: `zone_axis_admits` is identity for them (per-seat zones) and `Battlefield` has controllers.
5. `matches_target_filter_in_owner_zone` calls the live door with `OwnerZone`; `matches_target_filter_for_zone` already dispatches on its zone argument, so the mass and resolution seams call it with `obj.zone` and no list. `pub(crate) fn matches_target_filter_on_departure(state, object_id, from, filter, ctx)` is the new entry (a thin wrapper over a shared `filter_inner_with_lookup`).
6. `trigger_matchers::zone_change_clause_matches`: for a live entrant whose trigger origin is `Equals`/`OneOf` and whose destination does not name `Battlefield` (`destination_constraint_names(destination, constraint, zone)` over the trigger's `destination` and `DestinationConstraint`), the permanent is judged by `matches_target_filter_on_departure(.., record.from_zone, ..)`; every other live entrant by the plain door; every non-live-entrant by the record door. The branch needs only the record and the trigger definition, both already inputs.
7. `change_zone.rs`: the seams call `matches_target_filter_for_zone(state, id, obj.zone, filter, &ctx)`; the `scan_zones`/`origin_zones` lists remain only as the candidate population, never as a claim. The mixed tracked set (`TrackedSetFiltered`) is judged per member by its own zone, so no list can name a zone a member is not in.
8. CR: 400.1 (each player has their own zones; modified by the format), 400.3, 404.1, 108.4a + 109.5 ("you" for a card with no controller is its owner), 109.4 (no controller off the battlefield and stack), 603.6a (enters triggers read the permanent), 603.10a (leaves-the-graveyard triggers look back).

### 3.2 Unit B: count dedup with the three arms' reads (game/quantity.rs)

Dedup key and keyed population, modelled on `topology::shared_resource_dedup_key` + `players::aggregate_over_teams` (both exist: `grep -n "fn shared_resource_dedup_key\|fn aggregate_over_teams"` over `topology.rs` and `players.rs` returns one hit each; both stay read-only, and the key type is `PlayerId`, so no signature in them changes):

```text
fn zone_dedup_key(state, zone: Option<Zone>, player: PlayerId) -> PlayerId   // zone.map_or(player, |z| state.zone_storage_seat(z, player))
fn distinct_zone_holders<'a>(state, zone: Option<Zone>, players: impl IntoIterator<Item = &'a Player>) -> impl Iterator<Item = &'a Player>   // first-seen per key
```
`aggregate_over_teams` needs its own fold because its scalar is team-level; here only the population changes while the scalar stays a per-player extractor, so the keyed fold is `aggregate_over_players(distinct_zone_holders(..), aggregate, extract)` with no new fold function. `None` is the identity key (a scalar the player holds itself); `Some(zone)` is a container read.

Edits, all in `quantity.rs` (each arm's accessor read and its key are one edit):
1. `scoped_zone_holders(state, zone: &ZoneRef, scope, ctx, controller)`: `distinct_zone_holders(state, Some(zone.zone()), scoped_players(..))`. `ZoneRef::zone()` is the existing single mapping (read at `types/ability.rs`). Hand and Exile resolve to identity (Hand is per seat: `zone_storage_seat(Zone::Hand, s) == s` by Phase 6 3.1; Exile never reaches it).
2. `visit_characteristic_leaf`, `CardTypeSetSource::Zone` Graveyard/Library/Hand arm: `for player in scoped_zone_holders(..)` and the container read becomes `state.graveyard_of(player.id)` / `state.library_of(player.id)` / `&player.hand` (a small private `zone_container(state, zone, player)` shared with the next arm, so the three-way match exists once).
3. `QuantityRef::ZoneCardCount`, per-player zones arm: the same `scoped_zone_holders` and `zone_container`. The Exile arm is untouched (it already predicates per object, so it counts the object once).
4. `QuantityRef::GraveyardSize`: today `resolve_per_player_scalar(.., |p| p.graveyard.len())`. `resolve_per_player_scalar` keeps its signature as a one-line wrapper over `resolve_per_zone_scalar(state, zone: Option<Zone>, scope, ..)`, whose `Opponent` and `AllPlayers` arms fold `aggregate_over_players(distinct_zone_holders(state, zone, population), ..)`; the single-player scopes are unchanged. The arm calls the `Some(Zone::Graveyard)` form with extractor `|p| usize_to_i32_saturating(state.graveyard_of(p.id).len())`. The ~14 other callers of `resolve_per_player_scalar` are untouched (identity key).
5. `resolve_player_count`, `PlayerFilter::PlayerAttribute` arm (this is Phase 8's "all-seats count is Phase 10's dedup axis"; the parser lowers "for each graveyard with seven or more cards in it" to a count of players whose graveyard has seven, which on a shared graveyard visits one pile once per seat): a `BTreeSet<PlayerId>` declared before the `.filter(..)` chain, and the arm ends `&& counted.insert(zone_dedup_key(state, player_attribute_container_zone(attr), p.id))`, so the key is inserted only for a candidate that already passed `relation` and the comparison (relation `Opponent` over a two-seat shared pile still counts the pile once). `player_attribute_container_zone(attr: &QuantityRef) -> Option<Zone>` is `GraveyardSize{..} => Some(Zone::Graveyard), _ => None`; the wildcard mirrors `candidate_player_scalar`'s closed scalar subset (every other attribute is a per-player scalar with the identity key).
6. Not edited, with reason: `effects/mod.rs::matches_player_scope` (the other `PlayerAttribute` copy) selects the players who each ACT ("each player whose graveyard has seven or more cards, that player mills"); both seats satisfy the text and both act, so visiting seats there is correct, not a double count. `object_count_matching_candidate_ids` and `ObjectCount` stay raw and id-deduped (brief). The per-player journals (`SpellsCastThisTurn`, `PlayerCounter`) are not zone containers.

Observability of the dedup per arm (stated so no row claims more than it can show): the `CardTypeSetSource` consumers (`DistinctCardTypes`, `DistinctSubtypes`, `DistinctColorsAmong` into a `HashSet`; `Aggregate` through `seen.insert(member)`) already dedup by member or by set, so the key does not change any result for that arm; the shared helper keeps the three arms on one population authority, and the arm's discriminating row is the accessor conversion. `ZoneCardCount` with scope All (a sum) and the `PlayerCount` over a graveyard scalar are the two places where the key changes a result at a real card; `GraveyardSize` under `Max`/`Min` aggregates is key-insensitive (equal values) and under `Sum` (no supported card) is covered only by the building-block unit row.

## 5. Identity / provenance contract

Source phrase: "Anything referring to 'your' graveyard or library is referencing the shared zone." Authority: the zone the door states (the owner-zone door's object zone, `record.from_zone`, the departure door's `from`, or the filter's own named zone), and the seat set reading that zone's one container, derived each evaluation from `zone_storage_seat` (no stored copy). Binding time: live for the owner-zone door and the filter-named leg (`obj.zone` at evaluation); event time for the record door and the departure door (`record.from_zone` is a snapshot field of the event). Invalidation: none. Multi-authority hostile fixtures: (a) owner ≠ controller: a pile card whose `controller` field is the other seat still admits both seats at the owner-zone door (V4); (b) owner ≠ storage seat: a P1-owned card in the canonical seat's container admits both seats (V19 pile owner P1); (c) the same card on the battlefield admits only the seat that controls it (V4, V21 member, V14); (d) source seat canonical versus non-canonical (every runtime row runs with the acting seat P1 and the pile on P0); (e) door versus filter shape: the SAME pile card judged by the plain door with an unnamed leaf admits one seat, by the owner-zone door both (V4).
