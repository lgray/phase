A P0-owned pile card carrying a `PlayFromExile { granted_to: P1 }` grant is therefore listed by neither pass: zero times.
- At HEAD, caller P1 with raw `other = P0` lists that card once. So the exclusion removes a result HEAD produces, while 4.3 says the gate decision is "left unchanged".
- V7c says "lists it exactly once" and "P0 caller lists it zero times", and "count is 1 not 2 ... red at base". All three contradict the rule: after the exclusion both callers list zero; at base caller P1 already lists once (green, not red); the red-at-base case is caller P0 with a P1-owned pile card (raw `other = P1` is empty).
- Also 4.1's rationale ("non-canonical caller lists the pile twice") is incomplete: after a bare swap both callers double-list.
Required revision: make the class-L rule owner-keyed dedup rather than seat exclusion: iterate the DISTINCT storage seats of `state.players` once (dedup by `zone_storage_seat(Graveyard, p.id)`), read each through `graveyard_of`, and keep the per-card predicate `obj.owner != player` (the pass's stated meaning, "someone else's card"). This is the identity on non-shared formats (own-seat cards are owner == player and filtered; other seats unchanged) and lists a P0-owned pile card for P1 exactly once, and a P1-owned pile card for P0 exactly once. Rewrite V7c: caller P0 with a P1-owned pile card carrying the grant: red at base (zero), once after; caller P1 with a P0-owned pile card: once at base and after (reach-guard); P1-owned pile card for caller P1: zero in this pass (own pass owns it); Standard-format pair unchanged.
