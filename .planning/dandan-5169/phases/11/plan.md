## 3. Design

### 3.1 The carrier: the existing `EntryMods.performed_by` (charter claim 3, re-measured)

Measured by reading `game/zone_pipeline.rs`: `ZoneMoveRequest` has no recipient field (the struct holds `object_id`, `to`, `cause`, `mods`, `placement`, `exile_links`, `replacement_applied`, `face_down_in_exile`), so claim 3 holds literally. But `EntryMods.performed_by: Option<PlayerId>` ("CR 608.2c: the player performing the instruction that moves the object") already exists and already rides every leg the charter requires of a receiver carrier: `into_pending` / `from_pending` copy it to and from the serialized `PendingBatchZoneMoveRequest.performed_by` (zone_pipeline.rs and `types/game_state.rs`), the draw, placement, exempt and batch arms of `move_object_with_terminal` / `deliver_batch` copy it onto `ProposedEvent::ZoneChange.performed_by`, `execute_zone_move_with_applied_terminal` takes it as a parameter, and the event is serialized across a CR 616.1 pause (`pending_replacement`). Its one consumer today is the `to == Exile` arm of `deliver_replaced_zone_change` (CR 406.6 exiling player). Under this phase the same value is also the taker of a hand-bound card. This is one concept, not two: the player who performs "draw", "put into your hand" or "return to your hand" is the player whose hand receives the card (CR 121.1; CR 608.2c for effect-driven moves). No new field on `ZoneMoveRequest`, `ProposedEvent` or `PendingBatchZoneMoveRequest` is added, so `game_state.rs`, `layers.rs` (the two `ZoneMoveRequest {` literals) and `elimination.rs` (the two `PendingBatchZoneMoveRequest {` literals) have no forced edit, and the charter's Phase 5 seam note about `layers.rs` literal sites is not triggered (measured: `git grep -n 'ZoneMoveRequest {' -- crates` lists `zone_pipeline.rs:195` (the definition), `layers.rs` x2; none is edited).

The receiver is NOT derived from a cause source (charter claim 2): `ChangeZone` supplies `Some(ability.controller)` as `performed_by` at all four of its `execute_zone_move_*` call sites (measured: `grep -n 'Some(ability.controller)\|Some(ctx.controller)' crates/engine/src/game/effects/change_zone.rs` shows the pairs at the single-choice, random-single, `process_one_zone_move_with_terminal` and `ChangeZoneAll` loops, each commented "the controller follows the instruction, so it performs the move"). `ability.controller` is a field of the `ResolvedAbility`, so Haunted Fengraf's receiver survives its source leaving the battlefield as a cost. Pregame draws have no source: `ZoneMoveRequest::pregame(..)` plus `.performed_by(player)`. `change_zone.rs` therefore needs no edit.

### 3.2 The seam: `hand_entry_receiver` in `zone_pipeline.rs`, consumed by `deliver_replaced_zone_change`

`deliver_replaced_zone_change` is the single convergence of every delivery (measured: `grep -n 'deliver_replaced_zone_change(' -r crates/engine/src` shows the placement, draw and `execute_zone_move` callers in `zone_pipeline.rs`, `deliver` for the exempt, `Draw`-pause-resume and `engine_replacement.rs` paths, and the raw `ProposedEvent` builders `discard.rs`, `engine_debug.rs`, `triggers.rs` that carry `performed_by: None`). It already destructures `performed_by` and `to` from the post-replacement event. New private function in `zone_pipeline.rs`:

```
fn hand_entry_receiver(state: &GameState, object_id: ObjectId, to: Zone, taker: Option<PlayerId>) -> Option<PlayerId>
```

- `taker?`; `to != Zone::Hand` returns `None` (a `Moved` redirect that sent the card elsewhere therefore never rebinds, because `to` is the final destination of the event).
- `match state.format_config.format.hand_entry_ownership()` exhaustive: `OwnerKept => None`; `ReceiverOwns` continues.
- The origin test reads the object's CURRENT zone and owner (the move has not happened yet): the card rebinds only when `object.owner != taker` and `state.zone_storage_seat(object.zone, object.owner) == state.zone_storage_seat(object.zone, taker)`, i.e. both seats resolve that zone to one container, which is exactly "a shared zone" with no second source of truth for which zones are shared (Phase 6's exhaustive `zone_storage_seat` answers it; for Hand, Battlefield, Stack, Exile and Command it returns the seat itself, so two distinct seats differ and the origin gate holds). A card that is already the taker's own returns `None` (no journaled rebind, no churn).
- Returns `Some(taker)`.

The result is passed as a new final parameter `hand_receiver: Option<PlayerId>` of `zones::move_to_zone_with_entry_flags` at its single production call (the plain `_ =>` arm of the destination `match` in `deliver_replaced_zone_change`; the library-placement arm and the merge arm cannot have `to == Hand`). The other callers of `move_to_zone_with_entry_flags` (the `move_to_zone` wrapper and two tests in `zones.rs`) pass `None`. `zones::move_to_zone` raw movers, `DebugCommand` and every producer that does not supply `performed_by` keep `OwnerKept` behaviour by construction.

`ZoneMoveRequest::draw` gains the drawer as a parameter: `draw(object_id, drawer: PlayerId, seed_applied)` sets `mods.performed_by = Some(drawer)` (CR 121.1 makes the receiver intrinsic to a draw; the constructor's doc says it is the only producer, and its only caller is `draw.rs` (measured: `grep -rn 'ZoneMoveRequest::draw(' crates`), so omission is impossible and no builder call can be forgotten). The doc comment on `EntryMods.performed_by`, on `ProposedEvent::ZoneChange.performed_by` (`types/proposed_event.rs`, comment-only, listed in section 8) and on `PendingBatchZoneMoveRequest.performed_by` is extended by one clause: under `ReceiverOwns` the delivery of a shared-zone card to a hand also reads it as the taker.

### 3.3 The replayable install: one new optional field on `ResolvedZoneChangeCommand` (journal replay probe, brief S3)

Probe result (by grep and reading): `apply_resolved_zone_change` has exactly one production caller, `resolve_and_apply_zone_change` (zones.rs), which applies the very command it then journals; its only other callers are test modules (`zones.rs` tests, `cast_from_zone.rs` tests at `resolve_and_apply_zone_change` x2 and `apply_resolved_zone_change` x2, 34 integration-test references). So the live path IS the replay body ("resolve and replay install through one body"), the command is validated on deserialization (`zone_change_command_is_invalid`, `InvalidSerializedAuthority`), and replay of it is a tested contract. A raw `obj.owner =` write at the seam is therefore insufficient, as the brief says: the live applier would reject its own command, `command.owner` (the container key for both `remove_from_zone(from, owner)` and `add_to_zone(to, owner)`) would disagree with `object.owner`, and `zone_change_command_is_invalid` requires `command.owner == record.owner`.

Choice: carry the rebind in the zone-change command, not as its own journaled command. Reason (measured): a new `ResolvedRulesCommand` variant is an enum-variant addition with exhaustive-match sites in `resolved_commands.rs` (the `record_*` constructor, the two classification matches at the `ControllerOverride` arms, the deserialization validator) plus exhaustive matches in `tests/integration/cr733_resolved_commands_p2.rs` and `cr733_resolved_draw.rs`, while the rebind has no moment of its own: it is a property of the Hand-entry transition, and a separate command would need its own precondition that the next command re-checks. `ResolvedControllerOverrideCommand` is the precedent for a typed precondition-carrying command; the zone-change command already carries the same shape of precondition (`OwnerMismatch`). The field:

```
/// The owner the object had before this transition rebound it to `owner` (Hand entry from a shared zone under `HandEntryOwnership::ReceiverOwns`); `None` when ownership is unchanged.
#[serde(default, skip_serializing_if = "Option::is_none")]
pub rebound_from: Option<PlayerId>,
```

- `resolve_and_apply_zone_change(state, object_id, from, to, owner, receiver, record)`: new parameter `receiver: Option<PlayerId>` (the live `owner` parameter keeps meaning "the object's owner before the move", still checked against `object.owner`). `installed_owner = receiver.unwrap_or(owner)`; `rebound_from = (installed_owner != owner).then_some(owner)`; `destination_position_after_removal(.., installed_owner)` (the destination container is the receiver's hand); when rebinding, `zone_change_record.owner = installed_owner`, `zone_change_record.controller = installed_owner` and `zone_change_record.sync_trigger_source_context()` (the record is the event-time authority of the arrival and must agree with the live object; `retag_battlefield_entry_snapshots` is the precedent for retag-then-resync); the command's `owner` is `installed_owner`, so the existing validator equality `command.owner == record.owner` still holds without edit.
- `apply_resolved_zone_change`: the precondition becomes `object.owner == command.rebound_from.unwrap_or(command.owner)`, with `OwnerMismatch { expected: that value, found }`; the container operations keep using `command.owner` (Phase 6's `remove_from_zone` / `add_to_zone` send both seats' Library and Graveyard to the pile, so removing from the pile with the receiver's seat id is correct); after `add_to_zone` and `object.zone = command.to`, when `rebound_from.is_some()` a private `install_rebound_owner(object, owner)` sets `owner`, `base_controller = Some(owner)` and `controller = owner` (CR 108.4a + CR 109.4: off the battlefield a card's controller is its owner; `apply_zone_exit_cleanup` already ran with the old owner on the live path and the replay path skips it, so the applier owns this write for both, and `base_controller` must follow because `apply_zone_exit_cleanup` and `reset_for_battlefield_exit` compute `controller = base_controller.unwrap_or(owner)`: a stale `Some(old)` would resurrect the old owner as controller on the next zone change).
- `zone_change_command_is_invalid` (resolved_commands.rs): a `rebound_from` equal to `owner`, a `to` other than `Zone::Hand`, or a `from` other than `Zone::Library` / `Zone::Graveyard` is malformed (those are the two zones `SharedZones` can share).
- The receiver is applied only when the final `to` (after the CR 717.6 Attraction redirect) is `Zone::Hand`: `let receiver = receiver.filter(|_| to == Zone::Hand);` immediately after the redirect (zones.rs, `redirect_attraction_to_command` block, measured at `to = Zone::Command;`), with no `debug_assert!`. The Attraction redirect rewrites `to` to Command after the seam accepted `to == Hand`, so an assert in the raw branch (`from` or `to` is Command or Stack) would be reachable; the filter makes the raw branch receive `None`.
- Serialized-shape consequence: `ResolvedZoneChangeCommand` is serialized inside `GameState.resolved_rules_journal`. The client wire removes the journal (`CLIENT_WIRE_UNCONDITIONAL_FIELDS` names it) but persisted state and the P2P and server state carry it, and the Standing rule counts any serialized field: protocol 94 to 95 and wire 76 to 77 (section 6). Old saves deserialize (`default`) and an unrebound command serializes byte-identically (`skip_serializing_if`).

Facts checked so the install is complete (reading): `GameObject.owner` is read by `move_to_zone_with_entry_flags` as `let owner = obj.owner` before any mutation (zones.rs, `let owner = obj.owner;` in the body), so no stale owner survives inside the live function except the exit cleanup, covered above; library-knowledge facts (`ProductKnowledgeFact { owner, zone, identity }`, `viewer_knows_card_identity`) are keyed by incarnation and a Library-to-Hand move bumps the incarnation, so no `fact.owner == card.owner` comparison can span the rebind (Phase 6 plan 3.4's claim stands); `record_zone_change_library_knowledge_stamp` reads `record.owner` and Phase 6 resolves it to the storage seat, which is the pile for both seats.

## 4. Building Blocks, Logic Placement, Rust Idioms
