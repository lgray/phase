### Phase 23 — S10a: caster-relative shared-graveyard membership

**Goal.** Every "your graveyard" cast permission treats a card as the caster's when it sits in the container the caster reads, not when the caster is the card's concrete owner: under a shared graveyard, P1's flashback of a P0-owned Think Twice (also Deep Analysis, Faithless Looting), Lurrus's pool, a timed `CastFromZone` permission (Emry), Mayhem, Disturb's face enumeration and delve all work on the shared pile from either seat, and per-seat formats behave as before.

**Architecture decisions.**
1. **One membership authority, in the storage layer.** `GameState` gains one method answering "is this object in the `zone` container `player` reads", defined as `object.zone == zone` and `zone_storage_seat(zone, object.owner) == zone_storage_seat(zone, player)`: the existing shared-zone resolution, no new concept. For per-seat zones it reduces to `owner == player`, so non-shared formats are byte-identical. The three existing copies of the comparison delegate to it: `casting.rs::object_in_players_library`, the inline test in `effects/cast_from_zone.rs::looked_at_controller_library_cards`, and the shared-container test in `zone_pipeline.rs::hand_entry_receiver`.
2. **Every graveyard-cast owner comparison becomes the authority**, by symbol: `non_owner_graveyard_ids` (the "other graveyards" set is now the objects not in the player's container, empty under a shared pile), the owner skip in `graveyard_spell_objects_available_to_cast`, the all-`OwnGraveyard` exit in `non_owner_graveyard_permission_objects`, the graveyard disjuncts of the owner block in `castable_from_current_zone` (keyword admission and timed permission), `graveyard_keyword_routes_open` and `mayhem_castable_from_graveyard` (their consumers, method enumeration and preparation, read the predicate and need no edit), `GraveyardPermissionPool::admits` (`types/statics.rs`; takes the membership answer instead of two `PlayerId`s) and its caller `GraveyardPermissionSource::admits_card`, the graveyard-resident permission sources in `graveyard_permission_sources`, the Disturb face enumeration in `for_each_structurally_selectable_alternate_spell_payload`, and `GameObject::is_delve_eligible` (CR 702.66a says "your graveyard"; its doc says "owner's"). The class census is regenerable: `casting.rs`, `game_object.rs`, `restrictions.rs` and the AI candidate files by `awk` over `owner (==|!=) <ident>` within ±8 lines of `Zone::Graveyard`/`graveyard`, run at phase start with a positive control (the known sites must print).
3. **Kept as ownership, with the reason.** Hand, Command and Exile comparisons (personal zones; the format shares library and graveyard only). CR 602.2 activation from a graveyard (`ai_support/candidates.rs`, `ai_support/mod.rs`): the rule names the object's controller or, for a controller-less card, its owner (CR 108.4a), which is an explicit ownership requirement. `restrictions.rs` `CardsLeftYourGraveyardThisTurnAtLeast` reads the departure record's owner (a leave-count condition, not a cast permission, Phase 10's territory). Retrace and jump-start discard from hand (personal).
4. `PlayFromExile` objects tagged on a card in the shared graveyard keep their grantee test (`granted_to`), unchanged.

### Phase 24 — S10b: the caster owns what it casts or plays

**Goal.** Under the format's ownership override ("the owner of a card on the stack or permanent on the battlefield is considered to be the person who cast it"), a spell is owned by the player who casts it from the moment it is committed to the stack, and a land by the player who plays it, whatever zone it came from: a P1 cast of a P0-owned pile card through Future Sight, Magus of the Future or Bolas's Citadel (normal cast or a cast during resolution) is P1's, so Unsubstantiate, any owner-hand return and any later "owner" read use P1; replay of the journaled moves that follow reproduces it.

**Architecture decisions.**
1. **Generalize the one receiver authority; add no second mechanism.** `zone_pipeline.rs::hand_entry_receiver` becomes the single "who owns this object after the move" function: Hand keeps its existing shared-origin rule; a Stack destination makes the performer the owner (the only constructor of a Stack request is `ZoneMoveRequest::casting_to_stack`); a Battlefield destination does so only when the event names a performer, which only `handle_play_land` sets (every other Battlefield producer leaves `performed_by` empty, so a permanent that nobody cast keeps its owner — the format text names the caster, and silence about uncast entries is not extended).
2. **The carrier is the existing one.** `casting_to_stack(object_id, source, caster)` seeds `EntryMods.performed_by`; the land play seeds `performed_by` on its `ProposedEvent`. No new request or event field, so the pending-batch mirror and serialized shapes are unchanged. Both `casting_to_stack` call sites take the caster: the commit in `casting_costs.rs::finalize_cast_with_phyrexian_choices_inner` (one site for normal and during-resolution casts, since `initiate_cast_during_resolution` re-enters it through `continue_with_prepared`) and the `casting.rs::project_evoke_entry_state` preview.
3. **Install at the existing installation authority.** The Battlefield leg is journaled: the receiver reaches `resolve_and_apply_zone_change`, which records `rebound_from` and installs through `install_rebound_owner`. The Stack leg is the raw branch of `zones.rs::move_to_zone_with_entry_flags` (the generic command deliberately does not yet cover Stack routes); it installs the owner with the same `install_rebound_owner` and updates the arrival record's owner and controller as the journaled branch does. The commands that follow (Battlefield→Hand bounce, Battlefield→Graveyard) therefore carry the caster as `owner`, and replay of them from a post-cast state agrees. Journaling the Stack leg itself is outside this phase (a separate, existing first-cut limit) and is stated, not hidden, in the PR body.
4. **The axis is renamed for what it now covers.** `HandEntryOwnership`/`hand_entry_ownership()`/`ReceiverOwns` name hand entry only; the rule now spans hand entry, cast and land play, so the enum and method take a name that says the player who takes, casts or plays the card owns it. Not serialized (`HandEntryOwnership` derives no serde, verified in `types/format.rs`), so the rename changes no wire shape; `format_axis_census.rs` and doc comments follow. Per-seat formats stay `OwnerKept`: casting an opponent-owned exiled card in Standard leaves its owner.
5. `effects/bounce.rs` (the owner-hand return the review names) is verify-only: it requests Hand with no performer and routes to the object's owner, so it needs the fixed owner, not an edit.
6. Origin-independent by reading of the format text: a card cast from exile or hand by a non-owner also becomes the caster's. Idempotent for hand casts after Phase 11.

**Scope rule.** `crates/engine/src/game/zone_pipeline.rs` (`casting_to_stack`, the receiver function and its doc, the delivery call); `crates/engine/src/game/zones.rs` (the raw Stack branch of `move_to_zone_with_entry_flags`, doc of the `receiver` parameter); `crates/engine/src/game/casting_costs.rs` (the commit); `crates/engine/src/game/casting.rs` (`project_evoke_entry_state` only); `crates/engine/src/game/engine.rs` (`handle_play_land` seeds the performer); `crates/engine/src/types/format.rs` (the axis rename); `crates/engine/src/types/resolved_commands.rs` and `crates/engine/src/types/proposed_event.rs` (doc references to the renamed axis); `crates/engine/tests/integration/format_axis_census.rs`; a new `crates/engine/tests/integration/dandan_cast_ownership.rs` with its `mod` line in `crates/engine/tests/integration/main.rs`; the regenerated fixture.

**Claims to establish (measured).**
- Cross-owner library cast keeps the pile seat as owner, for each producer and for the during-resolution and land-play paths — probe `f1_*` in `dandan-run/scratch/c23-probe-engine.rs`; results recorded above.
- Stack-destination requests have one constructor: `git grep -n 'Zone::Stack' crates/engine/src/game/zone_pipeline.rs` near `ZoneMoveRequest`, and `git grep -n 'casting_to_stack(' crates` lists exactly the commit, the preview and tests.

A producer found that sets it is a finding.

### Phase 25 — S10c: AI mulligan progress on provably unimprovable piles

**Goal.** When the pile makes a free reveal futile, the AI stops taking it: for an 80-Island pile, a pile with at most one nonland, or any pile whose registered list cannot produce a hand that clears the free-reveal condition, both AI routes choose the issued Keep or a regular mulligan, so the mulligan ends and the native driver never reaches its action cap. The free reveal stays a legal engine option (human seats unaffected); no state is added and no budget is persisted.

**Architecture decisions.**
1. **The engine owns the improvability fact; the AI reads it.** A function beside `mulligan.rs::free_reveal_offered_to` answers whether any hand dealt from this seat's pile can fail the format's free-reveal condition, computed from the pile's registered list (`deck_pool_of(seat).registered_main`: public decklist knowledge, not hidden zones) and the format axis `free_reveal_mulligan()` thresholds, as a pure count: improvable iff the list holds at least `min_lands` lands and at least `min_nonlands` nonlands and the thresholds fit the starting hand size. The AI never re-derives the hand predicate (Phase 13's rule). A state with no registered pool (scenario states) answers "improvable", the current behavior.
2. **One AI progress policy, consumed by both routes.** A single function in `phase-ai/src/policies/mulligan/` decides whether an issued `FreeReveal` is worth taking (the engine fact above); `deterministic_choice` and `fallback_action` each consult it at their Declare branch and otherwise keep their existing precedence. Deterministic: futile ⇒ the registry's Keep, or Serum Powder, or a regular Mulligan (the card floor then ends the chain). Fallback: futile ⇒ Serum Powder or Keep, its existing non-reveal default. The deadline fallback stays cheap (it does not evaluate the registry).
3. **Stateless by construction.** The fix is a function of the format axis and the registered list; the free reveal's count is unchanged by the engine, there is no counter, no `PolicyMemory`, no wire field. This is the maintainer's constraint ("no general persisted redraw budget").
4. **Residual stated, not silently accepted.** A pool that can clear the condition with tiny probability (78 Islands + 2 nonlands: ≈0.7% per redraw by hypergeometric count) is improvable and is not covered; the probe shows it still fails to leave the mulligan within 60 steps. See the USER fork in the report.

**Scope rule.** `crates/engine/src/game/mulligan.rs` (the improvability function and its inline tests); `crates/engine/src/ai_support/mod.rs` (the re-export the AI crate reads, only if the function is not already reachable); `crates/phase-ai/src/policies/mulligan/mod.rs` (the policy function); `crates/phase-ai/src/search.rs` (the two Declare branches and an inline test module row set); `crates/phase-ai/src/auto_play.rs` only for a test row driving a custom pile to its stop reason. Compiler-forced consumers are standing-class.

**Claims to establish (measured).**
- Both routes take an issued free reveal at every step on a homogeneous pile — probe `c23_f3_*` (`dandan-run/scratch/c23-probe-ai.diff`): 80 Islands and 79+1 stuck 2/2 seeds, both routes, 60/60 steps.
- The fallback Declare branch takes an issued free reveal without evaluating the registry — read `fallback_action`'s `MulliganDecision` arm; and `deterministic_choice` takes it whenever the registry rejects — `search.rs` Declare branch.
- The 200-action cap converts to a driver fault: `git grep -n 'MAX_AI_ACTIONS_PER_SEQUENCE\|ActionSafetyCapReached' crates/phase-ai/src/auto_play.rs crates/server-core/src/session.rs`.
- The registered list is available at mulligan time for production loads and absent in scenario builders — `deck_pool_of` after `load_and_hydrate_decks` versus `GameScenario`.
- The default list's improvability and free-reveal behavior are unchanged: the 40-game histogram (0/1/2 free reveals in 34/4/2 games) is regenerated by the probe command and compared.

**Verification plan.** Production-loaded rows through `load_and_hydrate_decks` with a custom pile on the pile seat (the Phase 21 path): (a) 80 Islands and 79 Islands + 1 Opt, both seats AI, via `deterministic_choice` and via `fallback_action`: the mulligan ends within a bounded step count (red at base: 60/60 free reveals shown), and via the native driver stop reason is not `ActionSafetyCapReached`; (b) the free reveal is still issued and still accepted by the engine for the same hand (human option preserved: `legal_actions_for_viewer` carries it and `apply` takes it); (c) preservation: a varied pile whose hands qualify and can clear (a land-light list such as 15 Islands + 65 nonlands) still takes a free reveal (reach-guard: count > 0) and the default list's histogram is unchanged; (d) the improvability function's unit rows: homogeneous, one nonland, one land, exactly the thresholds (2 lands + 2 nonlands improvable), no pool.
