### Phase 23 — S10a: caster-relative shared-container membership; the caster owns what it casts or plays

**Goal.** Under the format's shared library and graveyard, a card is the caster's to use when it sits in the container the caster reads, not when the caster is its concrete owner, and what the caster casts or plays becomes the caster's. Every "your graveyard" cast permission works on the shared pile from either seat (P1's flashback of a P0-owned Think Twice, Deep Analysis, Faithless Looting; Lurrus's pool; a timed `CastFromZone` permission such as Emry's; Mayhem; Disturb's face enumeration; delve), and a spell is owned by the player who casts it from the moment it is committed to the stack and a land by the player who plays it, whatever zone it came from: a P1 cast of a P0-owned pile card through Future Sight, Magus of the Future or Bolas's Citadel (normal cast or cast during resolution) is P1's, so Unsubstantiate, any owner-hand return and any later "owner" read use P1; replay of the journaled moves that follow reproduces it. Per-seat formats behave as before.

**Architecture decisions.**
1. **One membership authority, in the storage layer.** `GameState` gains one method answering "is this object in the `zone` container `player` reads", defined as `object.zone == zone` and `zone_storage_seat(zone, object.owner) == zone_storage_seat(zone, player)`: the existing shared-zone resolution, no new concept. For per-seat zones it reduces to `owner == player`, so non-shared formats are byte-identical. The three existing copies of the comparison delegate to it: `casting.rs::object_in_players_library`, the inline test in `effects/cast_from_zone.rs::looked_at_controller_library_cards`, and the shared-container test in `zone_pipeline.rs::hand_entry_receiver`.
2. **Every graveyard-cast owner comparison becomes the authority**, by symbol: `non_owner_graveyard_ids` (the "other graveyards" set is the objects not in the player's container, empty under a shared pile), the owner skip in `graveyard_spell_objects_available_to_cast`, the all-`OwnGraveyard` exit in `non_owner_graveyard_permission_objects`, the graveyard disjuncts of the owner block in `castable_from_current_zone` (keyword admission and timed permission), `graveyard_keyword_routes_open` and `mayhem_castable_from_graveyard` (their consumers, method enumeration and preparation, read only the predicate; `graveyard_keyword_routes_open(obj, player)` takes no `GameState` today, so its two call sites gain the argument), `GraveyardPermissionPool::admits` (`types/statics.rs`; takes the membership answer instead of two `PlayerId`s) and its caller `GraveyardPermissionSource::admits_card`, the graveyard-resident permission sources in `graveyard_permission_sources`, the Disturb face enumeration in `for_each_structurally_selectable_alternate_spell_payload`, the graveyard land play (Crucible of Worlds class) wherever it compares owners, and `GameObject::is_delve_eligible` (CR 702.66a says "your graveyard"; its doc says "owner's"). The class census is regenerable: `casting.rs`, `game_object.rs`, `restrictions.rs` and the AI candidate files by `awk` over `owner (==|!=) <ident>` within ±8 lines of `Zone::Graveyard`/`graveyard`, run at phase start with a positive control (the known sites must print).
3. **Kept as ownership, with the reason.** Hand, Command and Exile comparisons (personal zones; the format shares library and graveyard only), and explicit ownership restrictions. CR 602.2 activation from a graveyard (`ai_support/candidates.rs`, `ai_support/mod.rs`): the rule names the object's controller or, for a controller-less card, its owner (CR 108.4a), which is an explicit ownership requirement. `restrictions.rs` `CardsLeftYourGraveyardThisTurnAtLeast` reads the departure record's owner (a leave-count condition, not a cast permission, Phase 10's territory). Retrace and jump-start discard from hand (personal). `PlayFromExile` objects tagged on a card in the shared graveyard keep their grantee test (`granted_to`).
4. **Generalize the one receiver authority; add no second mechanism.** `zone_pipeline.rs::hand_entry_receiver` becomes the single "who owns this object after the move" function: Hand keeps its existing shared-origin rule; a Stack destination makes the performer the owner (the Stack request has one constructor, `ZoneMoveRequest::casting_to_stack`); a Battlefield destination does so only when the event names a performer, which only `handle_play_land` sets (every other Battlefield producer leaves `performed_by` empty, so a permanent nobody cast or played keeps its owner; the land arm follows issue #5169's owner contract, which binds ownership at cast finalization and at `handle_play_land`). The membership test inside it is decision 1's authority.
5. **The carrier is the existing one.** `casting_to_stack(object_id, source, caster)` seeds `EntryMods.performed_by`; the land play seeds `performed_by` on its `ProposedEvent`. No new request or event field, so the pending-batch mirror and serialized shapes are unchanged. Both production `casting_to_stack` call sites take the caster: the commit in `casting_costs.rs::finalize_cast_with_phyrexian_choices_inner` (one site for normal and during-resolution casts, since `initiate_cast_during_resolution` re-enters it through `continue_with_prepared`) and the `casting.rs::project_evoke_entry_state` preview.
6. **Install at the existing installation authority.** The Battlefield leg is journaled: the receiver reaches `resolve_and_apply_zone_change`, which records `rebound_from` and installs through `install_rebound_owner`. The Stack leg is the raw branch of `zones.rs::move_to_zone_with_entry_flags` (the generic command does not yet cover Stack routes); it installs the owner with the same `install_rebound_owner` and updates the arrival record's owner and controller as the journaled branch does, so the commands that follow (Battlefield→Hand bounce, Battlefield→Graveyard) carry the caster as `owner` and replay from a post-cast state agrees. Journaling the Stack leg itself is outside this phase (an existing first-cut limit) and the PR body states it.
7. **The axis is renamed for what it now covers.** `HandEntryOwnership`/`hand_entry_ownership()`/`ReceiverOwns` name hand entry only; the rule now spans hand entry, cast and land play, so the enum and method take a name saying the player who takes, casts or plays the card owns it. Not serialized (derives no serde), so the rename changes no wire shape; `format_axis_census.rs` and doc comments follow. Per-seat formats stay `OwnerKept`: casting an opponent-owned exiled card in Standard leaves its owner.
8. `effects/bounce.rs` (the owner-hand return the review names) is verify-only: it requests Hand with no performer and routes to the object's owner, so it needs the fixed owner, not an edit.
9. Origin-independent by reading of the format text: a card cast from exile or hand by a non-owner also becomes the caster's. Idempotent for hand casts after Phase 11.
10. **Coverage contract (a design obligation on the plan, not a claim about today's code).** The plan carries discriminating, reach-guarded rows from both seats, through issued actions and the real payment pipeline, for: each gate of decision 2 (keyword route and its two consumers, static pool, timed permission, the all-`OwnGraveyard` scan exit, Mayhem, Disturb, delve, graveyard land play); each producer of decision 4 (Future Sight, Magus, Citadel; the spell on the stack bounced to its caster's hand; a cast during resolution; a land played from the pile top and from the shared graveyard); Control Magic changing the controller and not the owner; replay controls (the journaled Battlefield→Hand command and the land play's `rebound_from` serialize, deserialize and replay on their pre-states); the hostile row that a permanent put onto the battlefield by an effect keeps its owner; personal-zone and per-seat negatives (Standard own-library Future Sight and an opponent-owned exiled card keep their owner; an opponent-owned card in a personal graveyard, hand or exile stays refused; a P0-owned card under an explicit-ownership restriction stays refused). `non_owner_graveyard_ids_dedup_the_shared_pile` (Phase 8) is rewritten: shared-pile answer empty, per-seat answer unchanged.
11. **Owner of a played land: the caster.** Settled by issue #5169's contract (the Identity/Provenance contract binds ownership at `finalize_cast` and `handle_play_land`); the canonical-seat owner is an engine artifact, not a rules answer.

**Scope rule.** `crates/engine/src/types/game_state.rs` (the authority); `crates/engine/src/game/casting.rs` (the symbols in decisions 2 and 5); `crates/engine/src/types/statics.rs` (`GraveyardPermissionPool::admits`); `crates/engine/src/game/game_object.rs` (`is_delve_eligible`) with its callers `crates/engine/src/game/casting_costs.rs` and `crates/engine/src/game/interaction.rs`; `crates/engine/src/game/effects/cast_from_zone.rs`; `crates/engine/src/game/zone_pipeline.rs` (`casting_to_stack`, the receiver function and its doc, the delivery call); `crates/engine/src/game/zones.rs` (the raw Stack branch of `move_to_zone_with_entry_flags`, doc of the `receiver` parameter); `crates/engine/src/game/engine.rs` (`handle_play_land` seeds the performer); `crates/engine/src/types/format.rs` (the axis rename); `crates/engine/src/types/resolved_commands.rs` and `crates/engine/src/types/proposed_event.rs` (doc references to the renamed axis); `crates/engine/tests/integration/format_axis_census.rs`; a new `crates/engine/tests/integration/dandan_shared_container_casts.rs` with its `mod` line in `crates/engine/tests/integration/main.rs`; `crates/engine/tests/integration/issue_4999_treasure_cruise_delve_tokens.rs` only if the delve signature forces it (compiler-forced class); the regenerated fixture.

### Phase 24 — S10b: AI mulligan progress on provably futile redraws

**Goal.** When no redraw can improve the hand, the AI stops taking the free reveal: for an 80-Island pile, a pile with at most one nonland, and a seat whose redraw population has been depleted of the minority category by the other seat's keep, both AI routes choose the issued Keep or a regular mulligan, so the mulligan ends and the native driver never reaches its action cap. The free reveal stays a legal engine option (human seats unaffected); no state is added and no budget is persisted.

**Architecture decisions.**
1. **The engine owns the futility fact; the AI reads one boolean.** A function beside `mulligan.rs::free_reveal_offered_to` answers whether a free-reveal redraw by this seat can produce a hand the format's free-reveal condition would not offer a reveal for. It quantifies over the **redraw population**: what the redraw shuffles and deals from, that is `library_of(seat)`, the seat's hand and, because under a shared library the round closes with every declarer's hand returning together (`mulligan_timing`, `close_declare_round`), the hands of seats whose Declare entry is still pending. The count rule is the format axis `free_reveal_mulligan()` thresholds as a pure count over that population: improvable iff it holds at least `min_lands` lands, at least `min_nonlands` nonlands, and `min_lands + min_nonlands` fits `STARTING_HAND_SIZE`; futile otherwise. The engine reads hidden-zone composition and hands the AI one boolean; it reveals only that the redraw is futile, never a composition. The AI never re-derives the hand predicate (Phase 13's rule). Computed from live zones, so scenario states without a registered pool need no special case.
2. **One AI progress policy, consumed by both routes.** A single function in `phase-ai/src/policies/mulligan/` decides whether an issued `FreeReveal` is worth taking (the engine boolean); `deterministic_choice` and `fallback_action` each consult it at their Declare branch and otherwise keep their existing precedence. Deterministic: futile ⇒ the registry's Keep, or Serum Powder, or a regular Mulligan (the card floor then ends the chain). Fallback: futile ⇒ Serum Powder or Keep, its existing non-reveal default. The deadline fallback stays cheap (it does not evaluate the registry).
3. **Stateless by construction.** The fix is a function of the format axis and the live zones; the free reveal's count is unchanged by the engine, with no counter, no `PolicyMemory`, no wire field. This is the maintainer's constraint ("no general persisted redraw budget").
4. **Residual, disclosed.** A redraw population that still holds the thresholds is improvable and not covered, however low its hypergeometric clearing rate; the shipped rule is the provable one, and the PR body discloses the residual with its measured rate for the maintainer.
5. **Verification consequence (a design obligation on the plan).** The plan carries a case where the other seat has kept the minority cards and the futile seat leaves the mulligan (red under a rule computed from the registered list, which still reads the seat as improvable), and a case where the other seat is still pending with minority cards in hand and the seat is not declared futile. Plus: homogeneous and at-most-one-nonland piles on both routes through production load (`load_and_hydrate_decks`), the free reveal still issued and still accepted by the engine for the same hand, and a varied default-style pile whose hands can clear still taking free reveals with its histogram unchanged.

**Scope rule.** `crates/engine/src/game/mulligan.rs` (the futility function and its inline tests); `crates/engine/src/ai_support/mod.rs` (the re-export the AI crate reads, only if the function is not already reachable); `crates/phase-ai/src/policies/mulligan/mod.rs` (the policy function); `crates/phase-ai/src/search.rs` (the two Declare branches and an inline test module row set); `crates/phase-ai/src/auto_play.rs` only for a test row driving a custom pile to its stop reason. Compiler-forced consumers are standing-class.

**Deferral list.** None.
